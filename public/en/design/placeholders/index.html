Placeholders

2026

Placeholders is a series of paintings by Tymek Borowski, presented in 2025 at his exhibition at BWA Zielona Góra. The works refer to the form of internet memes that attempt to explain everything. In Borowski’s canvases, however, photographs have been replaced by abstract shapes, and the captions have no meaning whatsoever: they are sequences of random characters.

The catalogue contains all the paintings from the Placeholders series and an introductory text by the curator, Janek Owczarek.

Each painting features a typographical element. Tymek uses Arial, so the natural complement for the layout was another default font familiar from word processors such as Microsoft Word: Times New Roman. This is the first book in which I have used this popular typeface.

The structure of the publication is simple and clear. The catalogue of paintings is preceded by the introductory text, accompanied by photographs from the exhibition. As a rule, each painting takes up one page, with several full-scale enlargements inserted between them.
